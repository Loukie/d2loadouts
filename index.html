Sunrise Loadout Editor

Offline · edits your settings.json · nothing leaves your PC

1 · Load your character file

This is the settings.json Sunrise uses for your characters.

Drop settings.json here or click to choose

Your file is read in the browser only. Nothing is uploaded.

2 · Choose a Guardian

Your file holds these characters. Pick one to edit its loadout.

3 · Edit the loadout

Click Change on any slot. Pick from the list or paste a light.gg link — the codes are handled for you.

4 · Save

Download the edited file and drop it back where your settings.json lives.
